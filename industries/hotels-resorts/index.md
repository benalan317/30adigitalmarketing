Our hotel SEO helps boutique hotels, inns and resorts on 30A and the Gulf Coast win more bookings on their own website. We improve how you appear in search, in Google's hotel listings and in AI travel answers, and we measure the work in direct bookings and revenue, not traffic.

Many travelers find you on an online travel agency, then check your website, your reviews and your photos before they book. If your site is slow or vague at that moment, the booking goes back to the platform, and its commission with it.

## Where hotel direct bookings are won and lost

- **Your own name.** When travelers search for your hotel by name, an OTA ad or listing can appear above your site.
- **Google's hotel listings.** Rates, photos and reviews show in Google before anyone reaches a website, so the listing and its link to your official site need to be right.
- **Reviews.** Recent reviews on Google and Tripadvisor shape the decision, and travelers read how you reply.
- **The booking step.** A slow or confusing booking engine on a phone sends guests back to the platform they came from.
- **AI answers.** "Boutique hotel in Rosemary Beach with a pool" is now asked of AI tools, which name only a handful of properties.

## What our hotel SEO covers

### Technical foundations

Speed, mobile experience, crawling of room, offer and event pages, and hotel structured data, so search engines understand your rooms, amenities and location. We also set up tracking that follows a guest from search into your booking engine, which often runs on a separate system.

### Room, offer and experience pages

Pages for the ways guests actually choose: by room type, by occasion (anniversaries, girls' trips, golf weekends), by season and by what is nearby. Each one answers the questions guests ask before they book.

### Google and travel listings

Your Google Business Profile, Google's hotel listings, Tripadvisor and the regional guides travelers trust, kept accurate and complete.

### Reviews and reputation

A steady, policy-compliant way to ask guests for reviews, help replying to every one, and monitoring across travel sites. This is part of our [Digital Authority & Reputation](/services/digital-authority/) work, which also earns coverage in travel and lifestyle press.

### Visibility in AI travel answers

AI tools recommend hotels they can describe with confidence. Our [AI search visibility](/services/ai-search-visibility/) work keeps your facts consistent across your site, listings and press, and tracks how each tool describes you.

### Boutique hotel marketing that tells your story

For an independent property, the story is the edge over the chains: the design, the kitchen, the building, the people. We turn it into pages, press angles and guides that travelers find while they are still deciding.

Peak summer stays are planned months ahead. Spring break, fall weekends and winter each bring different guests: families, couples, golfers and longer-stay visitors. We plan pages, offers and refreshes before each window, and help fill the quieter weeks with the guests most likely to book them.

## Common questions about hotel SEO

**What is hotel SEO?** Hotel SEO is the work that helps your hotel appear when travelers look for a place to stay, and helps them book on your own website. It covers technical fixes, room and offer pages, listings, reviews and, increasingly, AI answers.

**How is hotel SEO different from hotel digital marketing?** Hotel digital marketing is the wider mix: search, paid ads, email, social media and metasearch. SEO is the part that keeps working after the spend stops. We focus on search, AI visibility and reputation, and work alongside whoever runs your ads.

**Can SEO replace the OTAs?** Not entirely, and it shouldn't. OTAs reach travelers who don't know you yet. Good SEO wins back more of the travelers who were already considering you.

**How long does it take?** Listing and technical fixes can show results within weeks. Competitive destination searches usually take several months, so we start well before peak season.

**Do you work with our booking engine?** Yes. We don't replace it. We make sure search and tracking work with it, and write clear briefs for your provider when something needs to change.
