Our mini golf marketing helps mini golf courses, go-kart tracks, arcades, oddity attractions and themed restaurants on 30A and the Gulf Coast reach families who are planning their days on a phone. We focus on search, reviews and clear pages, and we measure the work in visits, group bookings and calls.

Families on vacation decide late and fast. They search "things to do near me" from a rental porch or a rainy beach, check the reviews and the hours, and go to the place that looks fun and easy.

## How families choose what to do

- **They search by moment.** "Things to do with kids on 30A", "mini golf near me", "rainy day activities". Often the same day.
- **They check the basics.** Hours, prices, how long it takes, whether it suits little ones, and parking.
- **They read reviews and look at photos.** Recent reviews decide between two similar places.
- **They ask AI tools.** "What should we do with kids in Destin?" is now asked of ChatGPT and Google, which suggest only a few places.
- **Planners compare venues.** Parents booking birthday parties and organizers booking groups look for packages and a simple way to inquire.
