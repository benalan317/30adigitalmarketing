30A Digital Marketing works with established businesses on 30A and the Gulf Coast. We handle search, AI search visibility and your reputation online as one monthly program, and we report on the calls, inquiries, and bookings it brings in.

## Why We Started

The coast is full of businesses that do excellent work and are hard to find online. Their websites are dated, their expertise doesn't come across, and customers who should be calling them call someone else. We started 30A Digital Marketing to fix that for the businesses that give the coast its character.

Our connection here is family. We have roots on the Gulf Coast, and we built this firm around the places and the kind of businesses we know.

## Who Leads the Work

30A Digital Marketing LLC is led by Ben Slaughter, who has spent five years growing brands through search and content.

Many coastal businesses sell to customers who live hundreds of miles away, and compete with national companies for their attention. We bring the methods those companies use, sized to a business like yours.

## How We Do Business

- **Straight answers.** We tell you what we would do, what it costs, and what nobody can promise. Search engines decide rankings, and we won't pretend otherwise.
- **Your good name comes first.** No bought links, no fake reviews and no shortcuts that put the reputation you've built at risk.
- **Nothing goes live without your approval.** Changes to your website or your brand are yours to sign off.
- **Month to month.** No long-term contract. We'd rather keep your business by doing good work.
- **Published prices.** You know what each plan costs before you ever pick up the phone.
- **One point of contact.** One person who knows your business and answers your questions.
