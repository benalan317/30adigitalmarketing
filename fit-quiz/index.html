How the Quiz Decides

The quiz weighs your answers the way we would on a first call. It's a starting point, not a quote: every monthly plan is scoped to your business after a full audit.

  • Budget and size set the level. What you could invest each month, your revenue, and your team size point to Lantern, Beacon or Lighthouse.
  • Your website decides the buildout. No site, or one that's outdated, and customers who call or ask for quotes, point to the Website Buildout.
  • How involved you want to be decides Compass. If you'd like to run your own SEO and have a website, Compass is often the better fit than a monthly plan.
  • Competition and markets raise the level. A crowded market, or several towns at once, takes more content and authority work.
  • Your goals pick the services. Ranking on Google, appearing in AI answers, reviews, brand and using AI inside the business each point to a different service.

Common Questions about the Marketing Plan Quiz

Is the marketing plan quiz free? Yes. There's no signup, and you see your result straight away. Emailing it to yourself is optional.

How long does it take? About three minutes. There are 12 questions, and you can go back and change any answer.

How accurate is my result? It's a well-informed starting point, based on how each plan is built and who it usually suits. Your real plan comes from a full audit of your site, your market, and your competitors. To test the numbers yourself, the ROI calculator shows how many new customers each plan needs to pay for itself.

Can I combine plans? Yes. The monthly plans, the Website Buildout, and Compass are independent. Some clients start with a new site and add a monthly plan later, and Compass customers who move to a monthly plan skip the research and setup phase.

What if my budget is under $4,000 a month? Compass costs $5,000 to set up and $500 a month, and the free SEO audit costs nothing. Both are good places to start.

My business isn't listed. Can I still take the quiz? Yes. Choose "Something else". We work best with businesses whose customers find them online. We focus on the Gulf Coast, and national brands based elsewhere are welcome.

Do you guarantee results? No. Nobody can promise rankings or AI mentions. Search strategies usually take six months or more to mature, so plan on a long view.

What should I do after the quiz? Book a free strategy call. It takes 30 minutes and includes an audit of how your business shows up in search today. Bring your quiz result, and we'll start from there.
