## What is different about marketing in Destin

**One destination, two counties.** Destin is in Okaloosa County. Miramar Beach is in Walton County, just to the east, and it is often thought of as part of Destin. Visitors search for "Destin" and expect to see both. Each side has its own tourism body and its own bed tax district, so listings, events and press coverage are organized differently on either side of the line. The right plan covers both without confusing either.

**The bed tax reaches the whole county.** Okaloosa County voters expanded the tourist development tax countywide from March 1, 2022. It had been collected only in the south end of the county, including Destin. The tax pays for beach maintenance, promotion and local projects, and the county now markets a wider "Destin-Fort Walton Beach" destination. A business in Destin is competing for attention with that larger brand as well as with its neighbors.

**The water is the product.** Charters, pontoon rentals, dive shops and dolphin cruises sell trips, not rooms. Okaloosa County's artificial reef program has made the area a leader in Florida for reef activity, according to county coastal staff, which gives dive and fishing businesses a story that is true and specific. Trips like these are often booked through marketplaces, so the question is how many guests find you directly.

**Resorts sell more than rooms.** Golf, weddings and group events each have their own searches, and the Sandestin area competes with Destin proper for all three.

## How people search in Destin

- **By activity, then by place.** "Destin charter fishing," "snorkeling in Destin" and "Destin things to do with kids" start from what guests want to do.
- **By the name they heard.** Many guests arrive having heard of one resort, one restaurant or one captain. Your own name should return your own website, not a marketplace.
- **On a phone, on the beach.** Same-day searches for "dolphin cruise near me" and "where to eat in Destin" are decided on maps, reviews and photos.
- **In AI answers.** Travelers ask for itineraries, and the tool names the few businesses it can describe with confidence.
