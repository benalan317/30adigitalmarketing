## What is different about marketing on Alabama's beaches

**It is a different state.** Gulf Shores and Orange Beach are in Baldwin County, Alabama, and the Florida rules that shape the other pages do not apply here. Gulf Shores & Orange Beach Tourism markets the area, including unincorporated Fort Morgan, and guests inside either city pay a combined 16% in lodging taxes: 4% state, 10% city and 2% to the tourism body. Guests also compare these totals with Florida's, so listings and booking pages that show clear prices help.

**It had a record year.** Visitor spending on lodging rentals reached a record $923 million in 2025, up from $871 million in 2024 and more than double what it was ten years ago. The Alabama Tourism Department estimated 7.1 million visitors came to Alabama's beaches in 2025, spending nearly $6.8 billion and supporting 57,000 jobs. The resident population of the area is just under 27,000.

**Supply is growing.** About 950 new lodging units, equal to roughly 346,750 more available room nights, have opened or are opening, including Margaritaville Orange Beach, Abaco and Caribe Seaside, and hotels such as Hotel Rain, AC by Marriott and TownPlace Suites. More rooms means more competition for each search, and the properties that win are the ones guests can find and trust.

**Winter is a season of its own.** Snowbirds stay about 20 days on average, according to tourism officials, and their season ends in late February, just before spring break. Winter lodging rates are lower, but businesses say winter visitors help them stay open all year. Long-stay searches, such as "monthly rentals Gulf Shores," behave differently from week-long summer searches and need their own pages.

**Spring is strengthening.** Destination occupancy for March 2026 was up 7% over March 2025, according to the tourism body.

## How people search in Gulf Shores and Orange Beach

- **By city and by stay length.** "Orange Beach condos with a lazy river," "Gulf Shores beach house for a big family" and "monthly rental Orange Beach" are different searches.
