## What is different about marketing on the Forgotten Coast

**The name is a brand.** The coast is called "forgotten" because it is not heavily developed like many other Florida shorelines. Visitors come for quiet weekends and local seafood, not for crowds or high-rises, and a business can say so with pride. Marketing here works best when it describes the place as it is, not as a bigger resort town.

**It was rebuilt after a historic storm.** Hurricane Michael came ashore at Mexico Beach in October 2018 with winds of about 160 mph, the first Category 5 hurricane to make landfall in the Florida Panhandle. Recovery took years. Visitors who heard about the storm may not know what has reopened, so current, accurate pages, listings and photos do real work here.

**Visitation is climbing, and the area says it wants managed growth.** Gulf County's bed tax rose in the first quarter of 2026, with March collections up about 16% to more than $391,000, and the county's tourism council director says the goal is growth that keeps infrastructure in step with visitors, not a high-volume resort destination. Franklin County, which includes Apalachicola, Eastpoint and St. George Island, reported a record March, at just over $309,000. Statewide, Florida's first-quarter visitation was down 1%, so these two counties were moving against the state trend.

**The water is the draw, and it is changing.** St. George Island is a 28-mile barrier island with a state park, and Apalachicola Bay's oysters are central to the area's identity. Oyster harvesting in the bay has been restricted, and residents and state officials have discussed reopening it. A business tied to the bay should keep its pages current as the rules change.

**The search market is smaller, and the picture is shaped by fewer sources.** There are fewer competing businesses than on 30A or in Destin, so a few well-built pages, accurate listings and some press can carry further here. The flip side is that AI tools have less to work with, so one wrong or outdated detail can define a business.

## How people search on the Forgotten Coast

- **By town.** "Port St. Joe vacation rentals," "Mexico Beach condos" and "Apalachicola inns" each have their own results.

- **By season and event.** Oyster and seafood events, the holidays and fall weekends each bring different travelers.
