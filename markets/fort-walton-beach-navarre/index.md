## What is different about marketing in Fort Walton Beach and Navarre

**It is being noticed.** Expedia named Fort Walton Beach one of its top 10 destinations for 2026, the only Florida location on the global list, after traveler interest rose 45%. When a destination gets attention, searches grow before most businesses have updated their sites. That makes this a good time to be easy to find.

**Two counties, two stories.** Fort Walton Beach and Okaloosa Island are in Okaloosa County, part of the "Destin-Fort Walton Beach" destination. Navarre and Navarre Beach are in Santa Rosa County, with their own tourism body. Visitors move between them freely. Your pages and listings should place you clearly in the right one.

**It is a different feel from Destin.** Travel writers describe Fort Walton Beach as the family-friendly neighbor of a busier Destin, with parks, museums and aquariums. The Boardwalk on Okaloosa Island has restaurants, shops, live music and movies on the beach, and a fishing pier. Charters and pontoon rentals leave from Fort Walton Beach. That mix suits businesses that sell experiences to families and groups.

**Navarre is built around the water and nature.** Navarre Beach Pier, at 1,545 feet, is the longest fishing pier in Florida. The beach is also home to a Sea Turtle Conservation Center, Marine Park and a snorkel reef, and the Santa Rosa Sound offers calm water for paddling and fishing. These are specific, true details that make pages and guides worth reading.

**It is a place people live, too.** Residents and second-home owners hire contractors, clinics and professionals, so local businesses search for the same customers year-round.
