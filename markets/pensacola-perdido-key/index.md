## What is different about marketing in Pensacola

Events like these make for pages, press angles and lodging searches that peak on known dates.

**History and culture draw visitors.** Historic Pensacola Village, which includes 27 properties and museums, Fort Pickens and the National Naval Aviation Museum give hotels, venues and guides plenty to write about.
