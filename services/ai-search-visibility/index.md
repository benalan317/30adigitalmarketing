AI search visibility is how often, and how accurately, AI tools mention your business when customers ask for a recommendation. Our AI search visibility services improve both, through generative engine optimization (GEO) and answer engine optimization (AEO).

More customers now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews before they click a single link. Many act on the answer. If it names your competitors and not you, you've lost the customer before they reach a website.

## Try it yourself

Open ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity and ask the question your best customer would ask. For example:

- "Who are the best vacation rental companies on 30A?"
- "Which wedding venues near Rosemary Beach can host 150 guests?"
- "Who is the best roofer in Santa Rosa Beach?"
- "Recommend a med spa in Destin."

Are you named? Is the description right? Are competitors there instead? Your free strategy call starts with a full version of this test across every major AI tool. [Book a free strategy call](/strategy-call/).

## GEO, AEO and AI SEO: what the terms mean

- **Generative engine optimization (GEO):** shaping how AI tools such as ChatGPT and Gemini describe, compare and recommend your business.
- **Answer engine optimization (AEO):** structuring content so it can be lifted into direct answers, including Google AI Overviews and featured snippets.
- **AI SEO and LLM optimization:** broader names for the same work.

In practice, these are one program, and we run them as one.

## How AI tools decide who to mention

AI tools build answers from what they can find and trust: your website, search results, business listings, reviews, and what other credible sources say about you. They favor facts that are clear, consistent and confirmed in more than one place.

Each platform draws on different sources, so a business can appear in one and be missing from another. We measure each platform separately.

## What our AI search visibility services include

### AI visibility audit

We ask the questions your customers ask, across each platform, and record whether you're mentioned, how you're described, who appears instead, and which sources the answer cites. This becomes your baseline.

### Clear, consistent business facts

Your name, services, locations, prices and credentials, stated the same way on your site, your Google Business Profile, listings and profiles. The less there is to contradict, the less an AI tool gets wrong.

### Answer-ready content

Pages that lead with a direct answer, then back it up with specifics: plain headings, question-and-answer sections, tables and facts an AI tool can quote.

### Structured data

Behind-the-scenes markup that tells machines what each page is about, from your services and reviews to your events.

### Access for AI crawlers

We check that the crawlers behind AI tools can reach and read your site. Some sites block them without knowing it.

### Third-party corroboration

AI tools trust what others say about you. Reviews, listings, press and community presence supply that evidence, through our [Digital Authority & Reputation](/services/digital-authority/) work.

## Platforms we track

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot and Claude. We focus on the tools your customers use most.

## How we measure AI visibility

Each month you'll see:

- **Mention rate:** how often you appear across the questions we track.
- **Accuracy:** whether AI tools describe your business correctly.
- **Share of voice:** how often you appear compared with competitors.
- **Cited sources:** which pages and sites the answers rely on.
- **AI referrals:** visits and inquiries arriving from AI tools.

AI answers change often, so we judge progress over months, not single answers.

## A young field, handled carefully

AI search changes quickly, and anyone selling a guaranteed formula is guessing. We test, record what works and adjust. Strong [SEO](/services/seo/) stays the foundation, because Google's AI features draw on its search index.
