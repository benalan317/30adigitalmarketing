## We Take the Work off Your Hands

Once we begin, these jobs move from your desk to ours:

- **Deciding where the budget goes.** We give each dollar a channel and a target, and we move it when the results call for it.
- **Briefing and following up with vendors.** Your agencies, freelancers, and software providers work to written scopes and report to us.
- **Hearing out new sales pitches.** We review the platforms and agencies that call on you and bring forward only the few worth your time.
- **Reading reports from every direction.** You receive one monthly report that ties leads, cost, and sales together.
- **Keeping your staff on the same page.** We set priorities with your marketing team and check in with them regularly.
- **Reviewing work before it goes live.** Ads, pages, emails, and posts pass through us first, so your sign-off is quick.
- **Keeping up with search and AI changes.** We track changes in Google and AI search tools and adjust the plan as they happen.
- **Choosing new tools and vendors.** When a need comes up, we compare the options and recommend one, with the numbers attached.
- **Helping you hire.** We write job descriptions, screen candidates, and sit in on interviews for marketing roles, so the right people join your team.
- **Explaining marketing to your leadership team.** A monthly meeting covers what happened, what it cost, and what comes next, in plain English.

## What Stays with You

You keep the vision and the final word. We bring the planning, the follow-through, and the day-to-day management.

- **Your direction for the business.** Your goals set the plan, and the plan serves them.
- **Your approval on major changes.** Budgets, vendors, brand, and anything customers will see come to you first.
- **Your knowledge of your customers.** You know them best, and we build on what you know.
- **Your relationships.** Your vendors, partners, and staff stay yours. We work alongside them.

We work part-time, on a set schedule agreed in your quote. We can't promise specific rankings, mentions in AI answers, or sales figures. Search and marketing programs usually take six months or more to mature, and we report honestly at every step along the way.

## Your First Month and Beyond

The first month is for learning your business, your people, and your marketing as they stand today. By the end of it, you will have a clear picture of what you have, what to change, and how progress will be measured.

### Month One

1. **Research.** We study your market, competitors, customers, and current position in search and AI answers.
2. **Onboarding and access.** We collect access to your analytics, website, ad accounts, customer records, and social profiles. You keep ownership of every account.
3. **Team and program inventory.** We list every vendor, tool, campaign, contract, and cost, along with who handles what.
4. **Getting to know your team.** We meet each staff member and vendor contact to understand how they work and what they need.
5. **Structural recommendations.** We recommend what to keep, combine, change, or end. You make each call.
6. **Success measures and accountability.** Together we agree on the numbers we will track and who owns each one.

The month closes with a written findings report and a meeting to walk through it with you.

### From Month Two On

We lead the plan, direct the work, and meet with you monthly. Each meeting begins from a clearer picture than the last, as results build and the plan is adjusted to match. Every quarter, we review the full plan against your goals.

### Terms

The first three months run without a contract, so you can see the work and the effort before you commit. After that, a nine-month contract finishes the first year, and six-month contracts follow.

## Common Questions about Fractional CMO Services

**What is a fractional CMO?** A fractional CMO is a senior marketing leader who works with your business part-time. You get the planning, budget oversight, and team leadership of a chief marketing officer for a monthly fee, without the cost of a full-time executive.

**How much does a fractional CMO cost?** Our fractional CMO service starts at $5,000 a month. Each client receives a custom quote based on their needs and the complexity of the work, and bundling it with another service combines everything into one quote and one invoice.

**How is this different from hiring an agency?** An agency usually handles one or two channels and reports on its own work. As your marketing director, we look after all of it: the plan, the budget, every vendor, and your staff. If you also use our services, we lead those alongside everyone else.

**How much time will you spend on our business?** That depends on what you need, and we set it in your quote. Every client gets set working hours each month, regular check-ins with staff, a monthly leadership meeting, and a reply when questions come up between meetings.

**Will you replace our current vendors?** Only if you decide to. We start by working with the vendors you have. If one is falling short, we show you the numbers and recommend a course of action, and the choice stays with you.

**Is there a contract?** Not at first. The first three months run without one, so you can see the work and the effort before you commit. After that, a nine-month contract finishes the first year, and six-month contracts follow.

**Do you work on-site?** We work remotely. We don't keep an office on the coast, so your fee goes into the work rather than overhead. Meetings happen by video call at times that suit your team.

**Can we start with this service and add others later?** Yes. You can begin with leadership alone and add search, AI visibility, or authority work once the first month shows where it would help most. Adding a service later combines it into the same plan and the same monthly report.
