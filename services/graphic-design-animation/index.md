Our graphic design and animation service creates the visuals your marketing needs: brand graphics, social and ad creative, animated logos, motion graphics, and animated explainer videos. We scope each project to your business, so you pay for what you need.

Customers judge a business in seconds, and a clear, consistent look is part of how they decide. Motion graphics, which are designed graphics and text brought to life with animation, also explain things that are hard to say in a paragraph: how a service works, what a package includes, why one option beats another.

## What Our Graphic Design and Animation Service Includes

### Graphic Design for Marketing

Brand graphics, social posts, presentations, brochures, infographics and the graphics for your website and email. We work from your brand, or help you tighten it first with our [brand strategy](/services/brand-strategy/) service.

### Motion Graphics and Animated Logos

Short animated pieces for your website, social channels, and presentations: an animated logo, animated text, and data, and simple loops that catch the eye in a feed.

### Animated Explainer Videos

A short animated video that explains your service, process, or product in under two minutes. We write the script, design the visuals, and animate them. For a script and sales materials without animation, see [content and sales assets](/services/content/).

### Ad and Social Creative

Static and animated ads sized for each platform, so your [paid advertising](/services/paid-advertising/) campaigns have fresh creative to test.

## How We Work

Our in-house people lead every project, and we use AI-assisted tools to speed up drafts, variations and revisions. A person reviews each piece before it reaches you.

1. **Brief.** We learn what the piece needs to do, who it's for, and where it will appear.
2. **Scope and quote.** You get a fixed scope, timeline and price for the project before work starts.
3. **Concepts and drafts.** You review early directions, then refine.
4. **Delivery.** You receive final files in the formats you need, ready for your site, ads, and social channels.

## Pricing

Graphic design and animation are scoped project by project, and there is no binding contract. Ongoing work, such as monthly social graphics or a steady supply of ad creative, can be scoped as a regular package.

## Common Questions about Graphic Design and Animation

**How much do your graphic design and animation services cost?** We scope each project after a short brief and quote it up front, based on what you need and how many pieces. Ongoing work can be scoped as a monthly package.

**What is the difference between motion graphics and animation?** Motion graphics animate designed elements such as text, logos, icons, and data to explain, or highlight something. Animation is the broader term and also covers character and story-driven work. Most business projects use motion graphics.

**What is an animated explainer video?** It's a short video, usually under two minutes, that uses animated visuals and a voiceover or text to explain how a product or service works.

**Can you animate our existing logo?** Yes. We can animate a logo for your website, video intros, and social channels, and deliver it in the file formats you need.

**Do you design ads as well?** Yes. We create static and animated ads in the sizes each platform needs, and they work with our paid advertising service or with campaigns you run yourself.

**Who owns the finished files?** You do. When the project is paid for, you receive the final files and the right to use them in your marketing.
