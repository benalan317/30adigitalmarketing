Our paid advertising service runs your campaigns on Google, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and LinkedIn. We build the campaigns, set up tracking so every call, form, and booking is counted, and adjust each month based on what brings in customers, not clicks alone.

Paid ads are the fastest way to be seen, and the cost never stops: when the budget stops, so do the results. We run them next to your [SEO](/services/seo/), so the searches and messages that earn their keep in ads also shape the pages that bring in customers for free.

## What Our Paid Advertising Service Includes

### Google Ads Management

Search campaigns that show your business when someone is ready to book, buy, or ask for a quote, plus local campaigns and remarketing for people who visited and left. We build around the searches worth paying for and keep out the ones that only burn budget.

### Meta Ads on Facebook and Instagram

People still call these Facebook ads, and the same campaigns run across Facebook and Instagram. Meta ads reach people who weren't searching for you yet. They work well for visual businesses, events, seasonal offers and keeping your name in front of past visitors.

### LinkedIn Ads

LinkedIn lets you target by job title, industry, and company. We recommend it most for businesses courting affluent clients, such as wealth managers, law firms, luxury real estate brokerages, and private practices, where one new client is worth a great deal.

### Tracking and Reporting

Before any ad runs, we set up conversion tracking so you can see which campaigns produce inquiries and bookings. Each month you get a plain-language report: what you spent, what it brought in, and what we'll change next.

## Paid Advertising and SEO Work Better Together

Ads and search results answer the same question from different directions. We use ad data to learn which searches turn into customers, and SEO to make sure the pages those visitors land on are worth landing on. Ads cover the gaps while rankings build. Search strategies usually take six months or more to mature, so many clients use ads to stay visible in the meantime.

## How a Campaign Launches

1. **Weeks 1–2: review and plan.** We agree the goal, the platforms, the budget, and what counts as a result.
2. **Weeks 2–4: build.** Accounts, tracking, audiences, and the first ads. When new ad images or animation are needed, our [graphic design and animation](/services/graphic-design-animation/) team can create them.
3. **Month 2 onward: improve.** We test messages and audiences, shift budget toward what works and report every month.

We're a good fit for established businesses that already have a website worth sending people to, and that can set a steady monthly ad budget. We're not the right fit if you need a flood of leads next week from a small test budget. Ads reward patience, tracking, and a good landing page.

## Common Questions about Paid Advertising

**How much do your paid advertising services cost?** Paid advertising is priced separately from our monthly plans, and after a short review we quote it for your business. Your ad spend is separate from our fee.

**Which platform should I start with?** It depends on how your customers decide. Google suits people who are already searching. Meta suits visual businesses and keeping your name in front of people. LinkedIn suits businesses courting affluent clients and professional buyers. The strategy call helps you choose.

**Are Facebook ads and Meta ads the same thing?** Yes. Meta runs Facebook and Instagram, and its ad tools are now called Meta Ads. Most people still search for Facebook ads.

**What is PPC management?** PPC means pay-per-click, where you pay when someone clicks your ad. It usually refers to search ads such as Google Ads. Our service covers search ads, social ads, and tracking.

**Do you guarantee results?** No. Nobody can promise leads or sales from ads. We promise clear tracking, careful work, and honest reporting.

**Who owns the ad accounts?** Your ad accounts stay in your name, so you keep your data and your history if we ever stop working together.
