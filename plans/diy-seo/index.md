Compass is our DIY SEO workflow for small businesses. We set up an AI project trained on your company, brand and customers, connect your tools, and teach you to run it. After that, you publish content that follows SEO and AI search best practices, without having to be an SEO expert. It costs $5,000 one time, then $500 a month.

## Who Compass is for

Small businesses that win customers through organic content and want to run their own SEO, but aren't ready for a monthly plan. You need an existing website. You also need a little time each week to review and publish what the workflow produces.

Compass covers content creation. Technical fixes, link building, site changes and design aren't part of it. If you need those, see our [plans and pricing](/plans/).

## What the $5,000 setup includes

Setup takes about a month. You get:

- **Research.** An audit of your site and your competitors, and a map of the topics and keywords worth writing about first.
- **Your workflow.** An AI project, in ChatGPT or Claude, loaded with your company information, brand guidelines and customer questions, and set up to draft content the way you would want it written.
- **Connected tools.** Your accounts and the SEO tools you choose, connected and tested. Some steps can be automated, depending on how you want to work and which tools you want to pay for.
- **Training.** A session to walk you through the workflow, plus written guides for each task.

## What the $500 a month includes

Search and AI platforms change, and so does your business. Each month we review how your content is performing and recalibrate the workflow. We update it with your feedback and with what the results show, so the drafts keep getting closer to what works for you.

## How much you do

You decide how involved to be. The workflow drafts content to your brand guidelines and to SEO and AI search best practices. We recommend that a person on your team reads each piece before it's published, to catch errors. If you want a more hands-off setup, we can add automated checks that flag anything off-brand, but we still recommend review.

We recommend publishing fewer than 10 pieces a month. How many depends on how much time you want to spend reviewing and publishing. Sites can see results with as few as 4 pieces a month.

## Tools and costs

You pay for every tool directly: your AI subscription and any SEO software. The accounts are yours, and we tell you during setup which ones you need. Tool costs are separate from the $5,000 and the $500.

## What to expect

We guarantee that the content the workflow produces follows best practices and matches your brand. We can't promise rankings, because nobody can. SEO usually takes six months or more to show results, so plan on a long view.

## If you outgrow it

Compass stands on its own. If you later move to one of our monthly plans, we skip the research and setup phase, because it's already done, and take over running the project. What a monthly plan adds is ongoing research, strategic recalibration, stronger copywriting and closer editorial oversight.

## Common questions about DIY SEO

**Can I really do SEO myself?** For content-led SEO, yes, if you have a workflow to follow. Compass is built so you don't need expert knowledge. You'll still spend time reviewing and publishing, and results take months.

**Do I need a website?** Yes. Compass produces content for a site you already have.

**What does DIY SEO cost with Compass?** $5,000 one time for setup, then $500 a month. Tool subscriptions are billed to you by the tool providers.

**Is there a refund or a results guarantee?** No. We guarantee the quality and brand fit of the content the workflow produces, not rankings or traffic, and the setup fee isn't refundable.

**Can I cancel?** Yes. Compass is month to month. If you cancel, you keep the workflow, but you no longer get updates or recalibration from us.

**What isn't included?** Technical SEO fixes, link building, website changes, design and video. Compass covers content creation. We can scope the rest separately.

**Is the strategy call really free?** Yes. It includes an audit of how your business shows up in search and AI answers, and you keep the findings.
