<h2 id="faq-title">Common Questions about SEO Pricing</h2>
<div class="home-faq-list">
<details>
<summary>How much does SEO cost per month?</summary>
<p>With us, $4,000, $7,000, or $10,000 a month, depending on the level. Across the industry, prices vary widely with scope and quality, so compare what each retainer actually includes.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>How do I choose the right level?</summary>
<p>It usually comes down to how competitive your market is in search, the size of your site and how many services or locations you have, how quickly you want to grow, how much your own team can take on, and your budget. The <a href="/fit-quiz/">fit quiz</a> gives a quick suggestion, and we confirm it on the strategy call.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>Is there a contract?</summary>
<p>Not at first. We work month to month for the first three months. If you're happy with our work, we sign a nine-month contract to finish the first year, then continue with six-month contracts after that.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>Can I change levels later?</summary>
<p>Yes. You can move up or down as your needs change.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>Is the strategy call really free?</summary>
<p>Yes. It includes an audit of how your business shows up in search and AI answers, and you keep the findings.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>Can I combine offers?</summary>
<p>Yes, in any order. The Website Buildout, a fractional CMO, paid advertising, and design work can each run alone or alongside Lantern, Beacon, or Lighthouse. When you bundle a fractional CMO with a plan, you receive one combined quote and one monthly invoice.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What if a monthly plan is more than I can spend right now?</summary>
<p>Compass is built for that. It costs $5,000 to set up and $500 a month, and you run the content yourself. Tools are billed to you separately. If you later move to a monthly plan, we skip the research and setup phase.</p>
</details>
