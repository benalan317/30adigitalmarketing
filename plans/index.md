## How to Choose Your Level

Every level does real work. Higher levels add pace and reach: more pages written, more authority earned and more strategy time, so your search rankings, AI answers, and reputation start to reinforce one another. That combination is hard for a competitor to catch. Read how we [scope each package](/approach/), or see how many new customers each level needs to pay for itself with the [ROI calculator](/roi-calculator/). Not sure where you fit? The [fit quiz](/fit-quiz/) suggests a plan in about three minutes.

What usually decides the right level:

- How competitive your market is in search.
- The size of your site and how many services or locations you have.
- How quickly you want to grow.
- How much your own team can take on.
- How much you can budget.

## Why Our Prices Are Public

So you can tell whether we're a fit before you call. It also means the strategy call is spent on your business, not on negotiating a quote.

You'll find cheaper agencies, and some of them do fine work. Our prices pay for senior attention, a written strategy, and work that keeps paying off: pages that keep ranking and a reputation that keeps growing. We'd rather be clear about that up front than haggle later.

## Common Questions about SEO Pricing

**How much does SEO cost per month?** With us, $4,000, $7,000 or $10,000 a month, depending on the level. Across the industry, prices vary widely with scope and quality, so compare what each retainer actually includes.

**Is there a contract?** Not at first. We work month to month for the first three months. If you're happy with our work, we sign a nine-month contract to finish the first year, then continue with six-month contracts after that.

**Can I change levels later?** Yes. You can move up or down as your needs change.

**Is the strategy call really free?** Yes. It includes an audit of how your business shows up in search and AI answers, and you keep the findings.

**Do you offer local SEO pricing?** Local SEO is part of every plan, not an add-on. For businesses that serve a place, it's often the first priority.

**Are paid ads and design included in the monthly plans?** No. [Paid advertising](/services/paid-advertising/) is priced separately, and your ad spend is separate from our fee. [Graphic design and animation](/services/graphic-design-animation/) are scoped project by project.

**Can I combine the Website Buildout with a plan?** Yes, in any order. They're priced separately and neither requires the other.

**Can I add a fractional CMO to a monthly plan?** Yes. A [fractional CMO](/services/fractional-cmo/) starts at $5,000 a month and can run on its own or alongside Lantern, Beacon, or Lighthouse. When you bundle, you receive one combined quote and one monthly invoice.

**What if a monthly plan is more than I can spend right now?** Compass is built for that. It costs $5,000 to set up and $500 a month, and you run the content yourself. If you later move to a monthly plan, we skip the research and setup phase.
