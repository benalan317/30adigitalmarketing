The Website Buildout is a one-time, $10,000 package that gives a service business a new or redesigned website built to win inquiries. It includes the site, 10 optimized pages, a clear path from visitor to inquiry, and your business accounts set up correctly.

## Who it's for

Service businesses that win customers through quotes, calls and forms rather than a shopping cart: contractors and home services, professional firms, medical and aesthetic practices, and real estate. It suits a new business with no site yet, or an established one whose site no longer reflects the quality of its work.

## What's included

### A website built to bring in inquiries

A clean, fast, mobile-friendly site, structured so customers find what they need and search engines understand what you offer.

### 10 optimized pages

Service pages, location pages, FAQs and articles, written for search and for AI answers, in the words your customers use.

### A path from visitor to inquiry

Clear calls to action, quote and inquiry forms, click-to-call, and tracking that shows which pages produce leads.

### Business accounts, set up properly

Google Workspace for business email, Google Business Profile, Google Search Console, Google Analytics, and your social profiles.

### SEO from the first day

Titles, descriptions, structured data and a sitemap at launch. For redesigns, redirects from your old pages so existing rankings carry over. The approach follows our [SEO services](/services/seo/).

## What a website redesign costs

Website redesign costs vary widely, from template sites to large custom builds, and quotes are often hard to compare. The Website Buildout is a fixed $10,000, so you know the full cost before we start. To see what the site needs to bring in to pay for itself, use the [ROI calculator](/roi-calculator/).

## How the buildout works

1. **Discovery.** We learn how you win customers and audit your current site and competitors.
2. **Plan.** We agree the site structure, the 10 pages, and how visitors will contact you.
3. **Write and build.** We write the content and build the site. Writing follows the same standards as our [content and sales assets](/services/content/) work.
4. **Review.** You review everything before it goes live.
5. **Launch.** We launch, connect your accounts and check that every form and call is tracked.
6. **Handoff or management.** You take it from there, or we look after it for you.

## After launch

You choose what happens next.

- **Handoff:** you receive the site and your accounts. Your team can edit the site without a developer.
- **Management and maintenance:** we keep the site updated, secure and working for a monthly fee.

Either way, you own your website, your content and your accounts.
