**Effective date: October 21, 2026**

This policy explains how 30A Digital Marketing LLC ("we", "us") handles information when you visit 30adigitalmarketing.com or contact us through it.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** If you email us or book a strategy call, we receive what you provide, such as your name, email address, your business and website, and anything you write to us. Bookings are made through a Google Calendar booking page, so Google also processes the details you enter there.

**Information collected automatically.** When analytics is switched on, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. It records things such as the pages you view, how you arrived, your approximate location, your device and browser type, and your IP address, and it uses cookies or similar technologies to do so. We use this to improve the site, not to identify you personally.

This site does not have accounts, payment forms or contact forms.

## How we use information

We use what we collect to reply to you, schedule and hold calls, provide and improve our services and this site, and understand which pages are useful. We do not sell your personal information.

## Who else handles it

We rely on a few providers to run the site and the booking process, and they handle some information on our behalf:

- **Google**, for Google Calendar bookings, Gmail and Google Analytics. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **GitHub**, which hosts this site and may log visitor IP addresses as part of operating its service. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

We may also share information if the law requires it.

## Cookies and your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. To opt out of Google Analytics across sites, you can install the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). If you email us, you can ask us at any time to stop contacting you.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Email us at the address below and we will respond.

## How long we keep information

We keep emails and booking details for as long as they are useful to our relationship with you or required by law, and then delete them. Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings we choose in Google Analytics.

## Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above.
