Every engagement follows four steps: an audit, a strategy, careful execution and clear reporting. Plans run month to month with no binding contract, so we earn each month.

## What we believe

- **Strategy before tactics.** We decide what's worth winning before we do any work.
- **Measure what matters.** Inquiries, bookings and revenue where we can see them, not traffic for its own sake.
- **Be honest about what no one controls.** Search engines decide rankings and AI tools decide answers. We say so.
- **One program, not silos.** SEO, AI visibility and authority strengthen each other, so we run them together.
- **Earned, never bought.** No bought links, fake reviews or anonymous accounts.
- **Light lift for your team.** We do the work and ask for your time only where it counts.

## How an engagement works

### 1. Audit

It starts on the free strategy call, with a look at your site, your search and AI visibility, and your competitors. In the first month, we go deeper.

### 2. Strategy

A written plan: the searches worth winning, the priorities in order, and what success should look like at three, six and twelve months.

### 3. Execution

We do the work in priority order. Anything that changes your brand or your site goes to you for approval first. Where your developer builds, we write the brief and check the result.

### 4. Reporting

A monthly report in plain language: what we did, what changed, and what's next. Each quarter, we meet to review progress and adjust the strategy.

## How we scope each package

After the audit, we recommend a level: Lantern ($4,000), Beacon ($7,000) or Lighthouse ($10,000) a month. The level sets pace and volume. The mix of work is chosen for your business: a vacation rental company may need direct-booking content first, while a contractor may need reviews and local search.

We explain the recommendation and why. If a lower level will do the job, we'll say so. See [plans and pricing](/plans/), or test the numbers with the [ROI calculator](/roi-calculator/).

## How we use AI

We use current AI tools for research, analysis and first drafts, so more of your budget goes to strategy and judgment. A person reviews everything, and nothing is published without your approval.

## How we work together

You'll have one point of contact, a monthly report, a quarterly meeting and a shared plan you can see at any time. We are a remote-native agency and meet by video, with deep roots on the Gulf Coast.

## What we need from you

- Access to your website, analytics and Google Business Profile.
- One person who can answer questions and approve work.
- About an hour or two a month for questions and approvals, plus the quarterly meeting.

## Common questions about how we work

**What does "no binding contract" mean?** Plans run month to month. We'd rather keep clients because the work is good.

**Will you change our website without asking?** No. Changes to your site or brand are approved by you first.

**Can you work with our other agencies or our developer?** Yes. We often work alongside developers, designers and ad agencies, and we share what we learn.

**How soon will we see results?** The audit shows the quickest wins. Technical and listing fixes can show within weeks; rankings and authority build over months.
