Every engagement follows those four steps. The first three months run month to month, because we'd rather earn your business with good work than ask you to sign before you've seen it.

## What We Believe

- **Strategy before tactics.** We decide what's worth winning before we start any work.
- **Measure what matters.** Inquiries, bookings, and revenue wherever we can see them. Traffic counts when it brings those.
- **Honest about what no one controls.** Google decides rankings and AI tools decide their answers. We control the quality of the work, and we tell you plainly what's moving.
- **One plan, run together.** Search, AI visibility, and reputation feed each other, so one team runs all three.
- **Earned, never bought.** Every link, review, and mention comes from real people and real work, so it lasts.
- **Respect for your time.** We do the work and ask for your time only where it matters.

## How an Engagement Works

### 1. Audit

It starts on the free strategy call, where we look at your site, how you show up in search and AI answers, and who else people are considering. In the first month, we go deeper.

### 2. Strategy

A written plan you keep: the searches worth winning, the priorities in order, and what success should look like at three, six, and twelve months.

### 3. Execution

We do the work in priority order. Anything that touches your brand or your site comes to you for approval first. Where your developer builds, we write the brief and check the result.

### 4. Reporting

A plain-language monthly report: what we did, what changed, and what's next. Each quarter, we meet to review progress and adjust the plan.

## How We Scope Each Package

After the audit, we recommend a level: Lantern ($4,000), Beacon ($7,000) or Lighthouse ($10,000) a month. The level sets the pace and volume. The mix of work is chosen for your business: a vacation rental company may need direct-booking content first, while a contractor may need reviews and local search.

We'll explain why we recommend the level we do, and if a smaller one will do the job, we'll tell you. See [plans and pricing](/plans/), or check the numbers with the [ROI calculator](/roi-calculator/).

## How We Use AI

We use current AI tools for research, analysis, and first drafts, so more of your budget goes to strategy and judgment. A person reviews everything, and nothing goes live without your approval. We also set up private assistants for your own staff; see [AI systems for business](/services/ai-systems/).

## How We Work Together

One point of contact, a monthly report, a quarterly meeting, and a shared plan you can open any time. We meet by video, and you can email us whenever something comes up.

## What We Need from You

- Access to your website, analytics, and Google Business Profile.
- One person who can answer questions and approve work.
- An hour or two a month for questions and approvals, plus the quarterly meeting.

## Common Questions about How We Work

**Is there a contract?** Not at first. We work month to month for the first three months. If you're happy with our work, we sign a nine-month contract to finish the first year, then continue with six-month contracts after that.

**Will you change our website without asking?** No. Every change to your site or brand is approved by you first.

**Can you work with our other agencies or our developer?** Yes. We often work alongside developers, designers, and ad agencies, and we share what we learn.

**How soon will we see results?** The audit shows the quickest wins. Technical and listing fixes can show within weeks; rankings and authority build steadily over months, and the monthly report shows you the progress along the way.
